Q1: Why might we consider using Git?
Answer: We use Git because it makes collaboration and progress
tracking easier. Whenever a change is made to the code, Git stores
versions of the code in their repositories, so that everything is
tracked for everyone to see. Furthermore, when someone needs to share
code, they don't need to make a copy of their current and send it in.
Instead, they can just push their changes to the repository for the 
code to be updated in real-time to everyone with access to the
repository.

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Data science can be applied to practically any field that
collects data. A good example of this could be aerospace engineering.
When a satellite or rocketship goes into space, it collects all sorts
of data. Apollo 11, for example, collected samples of lunar rocks and
dust to bring back to Earth. Since it was a completely foreign object
at the time, scientists wanted to do a bunch of tests on the samples. 
Through data science concepts, we were able to not only collect that 
data and determine information about those rocks, but also were able
to determine what tests to perform on the rocks and translate the 
findings into something that NASA could actually do something with, as
opposed to just random tidbits of information.
